# Football

> Built 19.10.2026 (migration 163). Owner: "is ESPN data free? can we pull and create a football page? readonly", then "Under Media. One page … mostly Galatasaray, Champions League, World Cup, Euro … possible to enter new teams or new tournaments … It don't need to be a fancy SofaScore or FotMob app… Just simply showing games, tables, fixtures in a modern way. If possible; players information, club information … to be able to plan these things to my daily day life". Planning: "Subscribe on a team + plan it. So both".

Football had been dropped on 07.10.2026 because no free source covered current fixtures (API-Football's free tier stops at 2024). ESPN's site endpoints do, free and without a key — but unofficially: `docs/integrations/espn.md` has every endpoint, field and trap.

Favourite teams (26.10.2026, migration 168; owner: "A new football widget for favourite team(s), with the favourite selected on the Football page: how they're doing and the next match"): a star per followed team here, and one hook that gives the Personal page's Football card everything it shows (*Favourite teams* below).

## What is stored, what isn't

- **ESPN's data is never stored.** The browser reads it (`api/espnApi.ts`, the only file that calls ESPN) and TanStack Query caches it: tables and squads 10 minutes, fixtures 5, a match being played is read again every minute. Every answer goes through `footballModel.ts`'s parsers, which take `unknown` and turn anything odd into "nothing"; the page and each popup render inside an `ErrorBoundary`, and a failed read says what couldn't be read with a Try again.
- **The owner's choices are** (migration 163):
  - `football_follows` — a team (ESPN's number) or a tournament (its slug, `uefa.champions`), its name and logo as seen when following, a team's own league, `subscribed`, and `favourite` (168). A tournament can be neither subscribed nor a favourite.
  - `football_plans` — a match put in the day: ESPN's match id, its tournament, the **task** it became (the one `tasks` table), the kick-off and whether ESPN had fixed it when planned, and its origin (subscription or Plan it). Deleting the task leaves the row with no task (`ON DELETE SET NULL`), so the subscription never adds that match again; Undo links it back.

## The page (`/football`, Media & play)

- **Header:** Follow (primary), and once two things are followed, a filter row — All, then each team and tournament (`?f=team:432`).
- **Live** (only while something is played), **Coming up** (the next 30 days, by Norwegian day — "Today", "Tomorrow", "Wed 21.10.2026" — and inside a day by competition; the first six days, then "Show all N days"), **Results** (the last 10 days, newest first; four days). A match is one line: home | time or score | away, as football writes it; a small calendar mark under the time means it is in your day. Its details wait behind a tap. A page left open reads again every minute while a match is played or should have started, so it moves to Live by itself.
- **A followed team's match is never folded away.** A Champions League night has nine matches: when a followed team plays, its match shows and the other eight fold into "8 more" (opened in place); a night without one shows three and folds the rest; three or fewer show whole; the tournament picked in the filter shows everything (`footballView.byDayAndLeague`, checked against a real month in `scoreboard-cl-month.json`).
- **Tables:** each followed team's league, then each followed tournament that has a match on the page — a finished World Cup or Euro would otherwise show its old groups. A long table shows the top four and two rows either side of each followed team (`tableSlice`), with "Show all"; a group stage shows the groups with a followed team ("Show all · 11 more groups"). Zones are a dot in a theme tone (relegation red), their meaning behind "Key"; ESPN's own colours aren't used.
- **Following:** each team with its star (favourite), Subscribe / Subscribed, and ⋯ → Unfollow (asks, then "Galatasaray unfollowed · Undo"). A team opens its club popup; a tournament sets the filter. Unsubscribe and Unfollow of a subscribed team take its subscription's matches out of the day (*Into the day*).
- **Nothing followed yet:** the suggestions as one-tap buttons — the four the owner named (Galatasaray, Champions League, World Cup, Euro) and, since 25.10.2026 ("Add 5 top league"), the Premier League, LaLiga, Bundesliga, Serie A and Ligue 1 — and Search others. After the first, the rest are under Follow → Suggestions.
- Layout (`footballBoard.ts`, `verify-feature-boards`): one column on a phone; from the laptop the matches in main and tables + Following beside; Results gets its own track at 1920 and Following one at 2450. A match list is at most 40rem wide (W2).

## The popups

- `football-follow` — search ESPN (football only: teams and tournaments, the qualifiers are tournaments of their own), Follow per row; a team's row opens its club, followed or not (owner, 25.10.2026: "search teams and see their fixtures and be able to plan some matches"); suggestions before anything is typed.
- `football-team` — the club's logo and name, where it stands ("1st in Turkish Super Lig · W-D-L 6-1-1"), Follow — or, once followed, Subscribe and ☆ Favourite — On ESPN, its form (the last five results as W / D / L — each opens its match), **Coming up** — its coming matches in every competition, each with its day and competition, a tap opens the match, and Plan it beside it (or the item, once in your day; the first five, then "Show all N", `components/TeamFixtures`) — and its squad by position (number, name, flag, age). The club itself comes from its schedule answer (`all/teams/{id}/schedule`: standing, W-D-L, ESPN page, and its own league from the season's name) — ESPN's own club address stopped sending the header a browser needs on 31.10.2026, so the popup said "Couldn't load this club" (`docs/integrations/espn.md`, Traps). A squad needs a real league: the club's own, else the one it was opened with.
- `football-player` — number, position, age and birthday (DD.MM.YYYY), nationality, height in cm and weight in kg (ESPN gives inches and pounds).
- `football-match` — both clubs (each opens its popup), the score or kick-off, Full time / the live minute / Postponed, the goals by minute, the stadium and crowd, both line-ups with their formation (the bench behind a tap) once ESPN has them, and **Plan it**, or **In your day** once planned (opens the item).

## Into the day

- **Plan it** opens the one Add sheet (`useEntityModal` → `task`) prefilled: "⚽ Lille – Galatasaray" (home first), Norway's day and kick-off, two hours, the competition, round and stadium as notes, the area Personal and the slot category **Football** (migration `165`; owner, 16.10.2026: "should mark as football not personal" — asked 20.10: a Football category, the area stays Personal). The subscription's new matches get it too; matches planned before 165 keep "Other" until the one-off SQL in `docs/deploy-checklist.md` is run. The owner can change anything before Add; once saved, the match's record is written (`useRecordPlan`). A match with no fixed kick-off yet is prefilled with its day only. Plan it reads the records again first: a match already in the day (the subscription may have added it a moment ago) opens its item — "Already in your day" — instead of a second one; and the record never moves to a new item while its own item is still there (`recordPlan`; it says the match is now there twice). Before 25.10.2026 it did, which left the first item outside every record — one way to the duplicates the owner reported.
- **Subscribe** on a team: its coming matches go into the day by themselves — every match with a fixed kick-off in the next 45 days, once (`footballModel.syncPlan`). Each one is saved through `app_football_plan`, which calls the item save (`app_save_item`) and writes the record in the **same transaction**, under a lock per account and match — two devices running at once still make one item (`verify-db-football` races two sessions); Google follows as for any item (a time → Google Calendar). Subscribe says "Subscribed to Galatasaray", then the run says what it added ("Football: 5 matches added to your day", or that nothing has a fixed kick-off yet). **Turning it off takes its matches out** (owner, 25.10.2026: "When unsubscribed from a team it does not remove the events.. Now I have duplicates"; asked — "Subscription's matches"): Unsubscribe, and Unfollow of a subscribed team, ask "Its 5 matches coming up leave your day (and Google Calendar). Matches you planned yourself stay.", then say "Unsubscribed from Galatasaray · 5 matches removed from your day · Undo" (`useStopSubscription`). What leaves is `footballModel.subscriptionLeavers`: the matches the subscription added for that team's coming fixtures, not kicked off, still to do — never one you planned with Plan it, a done one, or one another subscribed team plays in. The items go through THE task delete (their Google Calendar events too), and their records go with them, so subscribing again adds them again; Undo puts back the items, the records, the follow and the subscription. With nothing to remove it just turns off ("Unsubscribed from Galatasaray"). The team's fixtures are read from ESPN for this (cached from the page); if ESPN doesn't answer, nothing changes and it says so.
- **A moved kick-off** (TV schedules move matches, ESPN fixes far-ahead times late): a planned match — subscribed or planned by hand — whose kick-off ESPN changed moves its item to the new day and time, **only while the item still sits at the old kick-off** (`itemAtKickoff`); one the owner moved (a replay at 22:00) stays where they put it and only the record learns the new time. The owner's title, notes, priority, Google choice and length stay (an item that blocks no time keeps blocking none). A closed item, or a repeat's day, is left alone. Postponed and cancelled matches are left as they are.
- **When it runs:** when the app opens or comes back to the front (at most every 30 minutes, `useAppBootstrap`), when the Football page opens (unless it ran in the last 5 minutes), and right after Subscribe is turned on (always — after a run already going, which may have read the follows before the change). Each match is tried on its own, so one failure never stops the rest. It says what it did once ("Football: 2 matches added to your day · 1 moved to a new kick-off"), as a warning when some couldn't be put in the day; nothing to do says nothing; every failure is in Developer → Errors. Planned matches outside the subscribed teams' fixtures are re-read one by one (at most 10 a run, cached like the match popup). While the app is closed nothing runs (iOS allows no background work) — the next open catches up.
- **"Never added again"** holds for a deleted item (a normal task delete leaves the record without its task). Undoing the subscription's save from Developer → Activity removes the record too, so the next run adds that match again; so does Unsubscribe (above).

## Favourite teams

- **The star** (`components/FavouriteToggle`): in Following, each team has a ☆ beside Subscribe; in the club popup a "☆ Favourite" chip sits beside Subscribe once the team is followed. A tap fills it (THEME's `highlight` tone — a category the owner flagged, as wishes and favourites are) and says "Galatasaray is a favourite" / "Galatasaray is no longer a favourite" (`useSetFavourite`). For a screen reader it is a toggle button (`aria-pressed`: it says whether it is on) named "Make Galatasaray a favourite" / "Remove Galatasaray from favourites". Several teams may be favourites; a tournament can't be one (168 refuses it). The list keeps its order — a star never moves a row. Unfollowing deletes the row, so its star goes too; Undo brings both back. A star is audited, so Developer → Activity can undo it like any edit.
- **The Personal page's Football card** (built separately, on the Personal page) reads one hook — the function a screen calls for its data — `useFavouriteTeamsGlance({ enabled })` (`hooks/useFavouriteTeamsGlance.ts`; `enabled: false` while the card is collapsed or hidden reads nothing from ESPN), which returns `{ teams, isLoading, isError, failed, retry }`. `teams` holds each favourite in the Following list's order, empty while none is starred (`FavouriteTeamGlance`, `footballGlance.ts`): `id` (the follow), `ref` (ESPN's team id), `name`, `logo`, `league` (its slug — open the club with `football-team`), `leagueName?`, `position?`, `points?`, `played?` (its row in that table), `form` (the last five results as `'W' | 'D' | 'L'`, the newest last), `results` (the last three with their scores, newest first — owner, 31.10.2026: "add last results as well"; a result ESPN gives no score for isn't listed), `next?` and `live?`. A match carries `eventId` and `league` (open it with `football-match`), `kickoffISO` (UTC), `day` and `time` in Norway's time (`time` null while ESPN has no fixed kick-off), `opponent` (short name), `opponentLogo?`, `home` and `competition?`; `live` adds `score` (home first, "2–1", as the page writes it) and `minute?` ("67'", "HT"). `failed` names the favourites ESPN didn't answer for (the rest still shows), `isError` means the follows themselves couldn't be read, and `retry` reads again whatever failed.
- **It says only what the page says.** It reads nothing of its own: the team's fixtures and results, its league's table (and, for a team followed without a league, the club's own league from its schedule) through the page's own cache entries and stale times (`espnQueries`), and a match being played is read again every minute, as on the page. What each team shows is `footballGlance.teamGlance`'s, built on the page's rules: `buildAgenda` (one copy of each match, the same live and coming-up windows), `outcomeFor` (the form the club popup shows) and `osloParts`. The next match is the first coming one not postponed (a match past kick-off that ESPN hasn't called live yet stays next for up to three hours, as in Coming up); checked against the real samples in `verify-football-model`.

## Decisions

- **Not stored, not server-side.** No edge function: ESPN answers any origin and needs no key, and keeping matches out of the database means nothing goes stale there.
- **A tournament can't be subscribed** — a whole Champions League in the day is never wanted; plan its matches one by one.
- **Unsubscribe and Unfollow take the subscription's coming matches out** (25.10.2026; until then they kept them — "those are the owner's tasks now"). Matches planned by hand stay: those were chosen one by one.
- **The assistant reads the two tables, never writes them** (`DB_CATALOG`: `ro`): it can't reach ESPN, so it couldn't follow or plan correctly.
- **A favourite is a team, and several may be** (168, 26.10.2026): a tournament has no form or table place of its own to show. Starring keeps the Following list's order, so nothing jumps under a tap.

## Not tested against

The real ESPN from the deployed site and the iPhone: the browser test runs the real app against the saved ESPN samples and an in-memory database. A live match's glance is checked only on a sample made live by hand (`verify-football-model`): ESPN's live answer hasn't been saved yet (`docs/integrations/espn.md`).
