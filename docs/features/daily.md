# Daily

## Overview

See Daily section below. Four modes (Day/Week/Month/Tasks) + a glance board.

## Daily Feature Detail

`DailyPage.tsx` (`src/features/daily/pages/`) — `PageContainer width="wide"` + `PageHeader`: the date arrows sit in the title with the greeting/time as subtitle, and ONE `SegmentedControl` switches periods. **Four modes** (`type Mode = 'day' | 'week' | 'month' | 'tasks'`); the Yesterday/Today/Tomorrow highlight is DERIVED from `viewDate` (the three old near-identical day views are one `DaySection`). Desktop tabs: Yesterday · Today · Tomorrow · Week · Month · Tasks; mobile collapses to a 4-cell segmented control (Today · Week · Month · Tasks).

- **Day mode is a PageBoard** (`dailyBoards.ts` → `DAY_BOARD`): phones/tablets and 1280/1469 stack the day card then the glance board; 1920 puts the card across main + one side with **`DayQuickRail`** (quick actions, up to 3 next blocks, the day's open / done — the Day plan header's own counts, repeat days still to do included — and an entry into Tasks) beside it and the glance board below; 2450 puts the glance board beside the day card (3 columns) and the rail under it as one strip. The day card = `WeekStrip` (7 fixed day cells for the viewed week, chevrons browse without changing viewDate, cell click = view that day; each cell's number = the day's open tasks + the repeat days still to do on it; the full `WeekWidget` lives under the Week tab) → `DayAgenda bare` — the day as ONE list (below). A new item from a day gets that day as its date and `taskRules.sectionForDay`'s section: Today for today, Backlog for any other day.
- **`TodaySummary` (the day at a glance; owner, 26.10.2026: "I should see basically a daily summary for anything I plan or do … only basic things … easy, understandable and clear design! We have done this before and it was really bad design")** — shown for EVERY viewed day, and every number follows that day (a past day reads as it was then; a day to come shows what is planned). From the top:
  - **The day line** (`summary/DayLine`) sits under the date in the page header, after the greeting ("Good afternoon · 15:36 · 3 of 5 tasks done · trained 41 min · 8,944 steps · 1,840 kcal · 2 episodes · read 12 min · Netflix renews"), so it shows at every width — beside the schedule too — and never moves the cards. Only what the day has, in that fixed order (`personalGlance.dayLineItems`), as a `MetaLine`; each figure is its card's, the task count the Day plan's own (`dayRowCounts`: a repeat's day still to do counts as open).
  - **Training** — a band the board's full width (`summary/TrainingBand`), six boxes (2 / 3 / 6 across by its own width): the day's session (done with its length, or its status and time from `sessionState`, or "Rest day" with the next session and a + menu that plans a routine for the day through `openPlanRoutine`); **This week** "2 of 4" (the owner's `training_days_per_week`) with Monday–Sunday marks (filled = trained, ring = planned, grey ring = planned and passed); **Average** a week over the 4 full weeks before it; **Streak** in weeks; **Lifts · 4 weeks** (the Improvement card's `computeLiftChanges` + `summarizeImprovement`: up / steady / down); **Plan** — the phase and the goal decision's pace ("+0.2 kg a week · Wrong direction", `useNutritionCoach().decision.pace`) and the goal weight; it opens the goal editor. Sessions are Hevy workouts + Strava activities (sessionsThisWeek's), the day's plans the calendar's (`useTrainingPlansByDate` + `matchDaySessions`), all through `personalGlance.trainingWeekGlance`.

Water.."): calories, protein, carbs, fat and water against the goal (the goal's calories and protein, the owner's own carb/fat goals or `cutDecision.dayMacroTargets`), the same eaten totals as Food · Today (`foodGlance.dayFoodTotals` over Food's `eatenTotals`: a planned row never counts; a day to come shows what is planned for it, labelled). Over the calorie goal is danger, the protein goal reached success (Food's colours). A tap or Details opens `summary/FoodDayPopup`: eaten / goal / left, the water tracker (its quick add — the one action there; owner: "look + add water"), the day's meals with their kcal (planned rows muted, "planned"), and "Open the Food page" (`/recipes?date=…` opens Food · Today on that day). Logging food stays on the Food page.
  - The glance board keeps its "Today at a glance" heading (visually hidden beside the schedule, as before).
  - **Six cards** in fixed slots, explicit column counts by the board's own width (`@container`: 1 / 2 / 3 at 36 / 55rem — 2 columns: Watching Football / Health Reading / Play next Shopping; 3 columns: W F H / R G S — every grid full, never a fourth column or auto-fill: a card never moves with its content, E2E-verified). **Watching** (`summary/WatchingCard`, replacing Watch next; owner: "what I've been watching, average time spent, and wishlist picks for a movie and a TV series"): the last 7 days and a day's average over 30, lengths estimated from runtimes ("≈", the year review's rule — `yearReview.watchesBetween`; the app can't know real viewing time), the two titles watched last (SxxEyy · date), and one released film + one series from the watchlist (`libraryItems` bucket wishlist), the same all day until Shuffle (`pickBySeed`); a title opens its popup. **Football** (`summary/FootballCard`): the starred team (chips when several) — its table place, points, its form (the last five as W/D/L), its last three results with scores ("W 0–1 at Gençlerbirliği · 17.10.2026", each opens its match; owner, 31.10.2026: "add last results as well" — the match the day box shows isn't listed twice), and the shown day's own match (its result once played, `footballGlance` `onDay`), else the live score, else the next match — only `useFavouriteTeamsGlance({ day })`; the table opens the club, the match its popup (Plan it is there). The table is always today's. **Health** (sleep/steps/energy/heart/weight, one box each — owner, 16.10.2026: "Not horizontal. Make them in a box"), **Reading** in boxes (the day's minutes against the goal, the streak) and the book in hand with "last read 25.10.2026 22:10" (Home's Books tile's rule, `currentlyReading`, up to the shown day), **Play next** (+ "Last played 26.10.2026 · …", one row of any library, `fetchLastPlayed`; never a hidden game — a launcher or tool Steam reports as played — since 28.10.2026, like the queue) and **Shopping** stay lists — a queue and a checklist read best as lists.
  - The figures are made of ONE box (`summary/cellKit.StatBox` / `StatValue` / `StatNote`: a small label with its icon, one big value, at most two short lines; a name from the owner's data wraps to two lines and shows the rest on tap) — the Health card's, which the owner approved. A card whose data can't be read says so in one line instead of showing zeros. Checked by `scripts/verify-personal-glance.cjs`.
- **Food on Personal (owner, 26.10.2026)** is the summary band above with its popup — not the old Nutrition card the owner removed on 16.10.2026 (meal timeline, quick-add, calorie ring), which stays gone; Home's brief still reads the day's nutrition (`useDayNutrition`).
- **`DayAgenda` — the day as ONE list (owner, 15.10.2026: "one item").** It replaced two panes side by side — Schedule (slots, repeating plans, Google events, with **Add**) and Tasks (`DayView`, with **Add task**) — in which a task with its slot showed twice and a day of a repeat could not be ticked. The rows come from the pure `daily/dayItems.ts` (`buildDayRows`, `verify-day-items`) over the reads the day already had: the day list (`useDayTasks` — nothing filtered, NEVER_HIDES), the one day schedule (`daySchedule.buildDaySchedule`: slots and yesterday's tails, repeating plans, Google events the app didn't make) and, by id, the tasks of the day's slots whose date is another day. Rules:
  - **Split by TIME, never by table.** Phone and any card under 54rem: **Anytime** first (all-day Google events lead it, then the open tasks and repeat days with no time — the open-wishes row sits above it), then **Schedule** (every row with a time, in time order, with the now-marker on today; "· Nh planned" counts only what holds time), then **Done** and **Cancelled** (untimed ones only). From a 54rem card (laptop): the Schedule column beside an Anytime column with Done and Cancelled under it (capped at 38rem / 34rem from 72rem). The parts are one DOM list; the right-hand wrapper is `display: contents` on the phone so CSS `order` puts Anytime first. An empty part leaves no line on the phone and keeps its heading ("Nothing at a set time") as a column.
  - **A task with its slot on this day is ONE row**, at the slot's time (the shared `ToDoItem` beside a time gutter: start, end, the slot's colour tag, an overlap ⚠). A task with a time and no slot sits at that time as a moment (no end — "at 14:00" holds no time, decision 3); a task with no time is Anytime; an overdue task's old time is not today's time (Anytime). A slot whose task is due on another day still ticks (its task is read by id).
  - **A day of a repeating plan is a row with the Repeat mark** and its own tick: tick = done for that day, ⋯ → Skip this day, and on a done / skipped day Not done / Undo skip — all through `useRepeatDay()` (it says "Done for 15.10.2026 · Undo"). A day nobody acted on is the plan's projection — since 156 together with its **day's task** (made on its day, `tasks.schedule_block_id` + `occurrence_date`): ONE row holding the task, its state the task's (done, skipped, missed), never a task row beside it; a day acted on is 142's one-off task + slot (`time_blocks.schedule_block_id` + `occurrence_date`, the plan's `except_dates`) and still reads as the plan's day, and a day's task moved off its day is a repeat row on the day it is now. A repeat with no time (155) is an Anytime row; a repeat at a time with no length is a moment. Tapping a day nobody acted on opens the plan's sheet with `occurrenceDate` (This day / All days); an acted-on day opens its task.
  - **A ticked timed row stays dimmed in its slot**; a skipped day reads **Skipped** there. **A day of a repeat that passed with no tick stays on its day as Missed** (never carried over — since 156 the database closes its day's task as missed on its own day: `cancelled` + `closed_reason 'missed'`, so it is neither overdue nor on Today's lists afterwards); the header counts it as "N missed", not open. Week and month counts don't count it either (a count can't see a training day a workout already covered).
  - **Training** rows keep the session's own status pill and ⋯ (`TrainingPlanMenu`) and open the session popup; their tick is the session's Mark done / Reopen (`useTrainingPlanActions`), disabled when a Hevy workout did it.
  - **Google events the app didn't make** keep their own rows with the calendar mark and no tick (they open `EditCalendarEventModal`); an event the app made is its row's (`daySchedule` de-dupes by id and `recurringEventId`). **A slot with no task** (made before "Convert old items", Settings → Subscriptions → Google) has no tick; ⋯ → 30 minutes later / Move to the next day / Edit / Delete (asks, Undo). The tail of yesterday's slot or plan after midnight is a "continued" row that opens the same thing.
  - **One Add** in the header (and Morning / Afternoon / Evening chips on an empty day) opens the one sheet (`useEntityModal().open({ kind: 'task', defaults })`) with the day as its date (`date`, `dueDate`, the day's section; a chip adds `startTime` + `scheduled`). The header: "Day plan", N open (the day list's open tasks + the repeat days still to do that are not tasks yet — `repeatDayKeys`, so today's repeats count once), N done, N missed, and "Next: … at HH:MM" / "Now: … until HH:MM" from `daySchedule.aheadOnDay`; the Google sync button when Calendar is connected.
  - Rows are 44px for one line (the tick's and ⋯'s tap areas reach into the row padding); custom rows copy `ToDoItem`'s anatomy (tick · title · pills · ⋯) so the list reads as one. The old Night group and the "No time set" group are gone: the gutter shows 03:00 as it is, and a slot with no time is Anytime.
- **Week and Month** share `PICKER_SECTIONS`: picker (40rem — `WeekWidget` / `MonthWidget big`) + the picked day in place — one pane (picked day or Upcoming) at 1280/1469, the picked day's list + Upcoming at 1920, the picked day two tracks wide at 2450 (its list is the one day list — the separate "Tasks · <day>" card went with the merge, 15.10.2026); on phones/tablets the week strip opens the day, on wider pages it picks in place. Every day number and the week's open / % done include the repeat days still to do from today on (a ticked one is its own task); the month puts a dot on every day a repeat falls on (a missed one too) and its "N open" adds the month's repeat days still to do. The picked-day card shortens its date when it has two buttons; Daily's header keeps one size in every section: as wide as the widest section's board (`sectionsWidthRem`), with the date's day line under it in every section too — it used to stop at each section's own board and drop the line outside the day, so the switcher jumped on every tab (owner, 31.10.2026: "the layout should not move while navigating between sections"). **Tasks mode** = `TasksPanel` (all tasks): one card on phones, columns on wider pages — overdue · open now · today | upcoming | no date | done today (`taskRules.taskGroups`; Today = the day pane's own list, so an undated task in the Today section is under Today, not No date); an empty column keeps its heading with a 0 badge.
- **Cancel** (⊘, `ToDoItem`/`WorkTaskCard`) sets `status='cancelled'` — `TaskStatus` always had the value but no UI ever set it; distinct from Delete (keeps a record instead of destroying the row). Daily's day list has a "Cancelled" part (cancelled on that day, like Done; a timed one stays dimmed in its slot) so a cancelled task stays visible; the Work board has no cancelled column, so there it simply drops off (matches the "cancelled excluded from active counts" convention used everywhere — WeekWidget/MonthWidget/HomePage/…).
- **`ToDoItem`'s desktop action row (✎/↳/📋/⊘/✕) and the checkbox both silently no-opped into opening the edit modal instead — a real bug in `useSwipeToReveal.ts`, not in `ToDoItem` itself.** `onPointerDown` called `e.currentTarget.setPointerCapture(e.pointerId)` **unconditionally**, on every pointerdown anywhere inside the row — including one that started on a nested interactive child (pointerdown bubbles up regardless of which descendant it began on). Once a pointer is captured, the browser retargets that pointer's subsequent `pointerup` **and the `click` it synthesizes** to the CAPTURING element (the row), never wherever the pointer physically is — so a plain tap on the checkbox or any of the five action buttons fired the ROW's own `onClick` (open edit) instead of the button's own handler: the checkbox never toggled, Delete never deleted, Cancel/Reopen never updated status, and every action looked identical from the outside ("everything opens edit"). The mobile `⋯` menu was unaffected because Headless UI's `MenuItems` portals outside the row's DOM subtree, so its clicks never bubble through this handler at all — this made the bug desktop-only, exactly where it was reported. Fixed by deferring `setPointerCapture` out of `onPointerDown` and into `onPointerMove`, firing only once a genuine horizontal swipe is confirmed (`axisLocked.current === 'x'`) — a plain tap never moves enough to reach that branch, so its click reaches its real target untouched; only an actual swipe gesture (which still needs capture to survive the pointer leaving the row's bounds mid-gesture) ever captures the pointer. `ToDoItem` opens its editor as the `task` popup (rows no longer each mount a hidden editor), keeps its action menus in `ToDoItemActions.tsx`, and toasts nothing itself — the migrated task hooks own error feedback (Delete asks, then says "Task deleted · Undo" — `docs/features/delete-undo.md`).
- **The `section` day-leak (real bug, fixed on the READ side only).** Daily once mapped a whole range of days onto one `section` (+2…+7 → `this_week`, the rest → `backlog`) and read undated rows of that section on each of them, so one undated row showed on six days. Only Today keeps undated rows now (`taskRules.onDay`; see `docs/features/tasks.md` → **One task model**); no row was rewritten and `section` stays (dropping it breaks 037's audit list, 046's policies and `DB_CATALOG`, and both possible backfills manufacture wrong dates). Undated rows still reach the Tasks tab's "No date" group, the open undated ones of this week the day list's folded "This week — no date" band (`DayAgendaExtras.tsx`). `TaskTab`'s `SECTIONS` also dropped the `Tomorrow`/`This Week` pills (neither has a home of its own — picking one only made a task float like Backlog under a date-shaped name). Since 13.10.2026 the app no longer writes either value: `taskRules.sectionForDay` (a new task from Daily's day view) and `planModal.config`'s `sectionForDate` ("Also add to Tasks", Training's planned task) give Today for today and Backlog for any other day — a new task from the Tomorrow tab used to open with "Tomorrow (legacy)" selected. Backlog keeps a future task out of today's server-side `section.eq.today` lists exactly as Tomorrow / This week did; on its day it shows by its due date. Since 14.10.2026 (owner: "Remove that Tomorrow button") there is no "(legacy)" pill either: a row still holding a retired value opens with Backlog picked and Save writes Backlog (`taskRules.liveSection`); the planner's form, its defaults and the task create/update inputs take `LiveTaskSection` (`inbox|today|backlog`), so no caller can pass one, and the assistant's `db_insert`/`db_update` refuse them (its "plan a watch" tool files Today/Backlog since 13.10.2026). **A new item's date must keep defaulting to today** (`planForm.ts`; since one item, 155, the section follows the date — no date → Inbox) — four server-side `section.eq.today` predicates (`phone-gateway` ×2, `push-send`, `ai-proxy`) drain silently if new items land undated in the Inbox, quietening the brief and the lock-screen push forever.
- **The viewed day follows the date (28.10.2026).** Personal and Food keep showing today when the installed app wakes up the next day (`shared/hooks/useToday.useFollowToday`): before, a page left on Today overnight showed yesterday and logged water and food there. A day picked on purpose stays.
- **Diary (owner, 29.10.2026: "what where with whom and when I have eaten, watched, played, trained, planned, done tasks… Like a diary. Also a diary text input where I speak about my day").** Personal → **Diary** (a view next to Tasks; ‹ › page through days, the header keeps the day line). Two cards (`features/diary/components/DiaryView.tsx`, `dailyBoards.DIARY_BOARD`: stacked on a phone, the day's list beside the words from step 2):
  - **Your day** — the owner's own words, one per day (`diary_entries`, migration 170: unique per day, audited, owner lock). It saves by itself 2 s after typing stops and when the box loses focus (the card's subtitle says "Saving…", "Saved 29.10.2026 21:14", "Not saved yet", or "Not saved" with Retry after an error — no toast for each save). Unsaved words wait per day on the device (`diary/diaryDrafts.ts`): a mode switch, iOS reloading the app or the midnight move to the next day never loses them, and text typed during a save stays. While a day's words fail to load, the box is locked (a save would replace them) with Retry. Dictation is the iPhone keyboard's microphone. The text stays out of the error log (`logPayload: false`). Ask AI can read it, never write it (`DB_CATALOG` `ro`: a prompt rule alone can't stop a replace).
  - **What happened** — read from each feature, never copied (`diaryTimeline.buildDiary`, `verify-diary-timeline`): meals by meal (Food's own `mealsBySlot`: its names and eaten kcal, every food listed, up to two lines), Hevy workouts, watched episodes and films, reading per book (minutes), the game last played that day (only the last one — Games keeps no per-day play log), tasks done (their `completed_at`), and planned slots with a time (done, Missed for a repeat's day that passed unticked, else Skipped — as Daily reads them). One thing is one row: a slot and its done task show once at the slot, a session that synced shows once as Trained, a done episode task already Watched isn't shown again. A detail says only what the icon doesn't (minutes, S03E04, missed). Clock times are the device's (the owner's phone is on Oslo time); a meal sits at the time it was logged (181 — one row per time, two snacks two rows), a log from before 181 at its meal's usual hour with no time. Every source loads before the list shows, and one that fails is named ("Couldn't load: Food") instead of reading as nothing. Reading older than about 400 days isn't in the window Books shares. "Where" and "with whom" aren't recorded anywhere yet — they go in the words for now.
  - Nothing is ever cleared: past days stay as they were (owner, 29.10.2026).
