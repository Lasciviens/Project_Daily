# Shop

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 13.10.2026 (word for word, apart from translated quotes). This file describes how the feature works and why. The rules Claude must follow while changing it are in `.claude/rules/shop.md`, which Claude Code loads by itself when a matching file is read or edited.

## Overview

See Shop section below. Wishlist (general wishes + models, deals, the Prisjakt price watch) · Quick list (Kassalapp chain prices) · Owned (what you own and owned, money chains, Try other prices, Stats). Its own nav entry (sidebar, More sheet).

## Shop Feature Detail
`/#/shop` → `ShopPage` (`src/features/shop/`), rebuilt 08.10.2026 (migration `137`) into three views, `?view=` (`bought` opens Owned): **Wishlist · Quick list · Owned** (Owned has **Things | Stats**, `?tab=stats`). Phones show no counts in the view control. The header bar keeps one row and the view switch one width on every view (owner, 03.11.2026: a tab switch never reshapes the bar): on a phone Owned's Things | Stats and its "More ways to add" menu sit in the row right under the bar (they used to add a row to it and narrow the switch); from a tablet they share the bar's row. A wishlist row lives a life: **To buy** (`status 'wishlist'`) → **Mine** (`'bought'`) → **Sold or gone** (`'bought'` + `disposal` facts). A POSSESSION = `list='wishlist' AND kind='item' AND status='bought' AND kept` (`ownModel.isPossession`); quick-list ticks are never possessions.
- **Categories** (Wishlist and Owned, `CategorySection`): sections that fold ("Fold all"; remembered per device, `useCollapsedGroups`, `lasci.shop.collapsed.<view>`), each one aligned grid (rows stretch so prices and buttons line up — owner, 08.10.2026) with **each subcategory named once** (09.11.2026, approved with the tiles 10.11.2026; it used to sit above every card). Owned: compact rows (step A, 10.11.2026), each subcategory named once above its own card of rows, a wide list dealing its categories into column stacks (`ownedRow.rowStacks`: 21rem and up a stack, at most 40rem — one on a phone, two at 1469 and sideways, four at 2450; never more stacks than categories). Wishlist: picture tiles (`PackedTiles`), a small subcategory sharing the row of the one before when it fits (named once, above its own tiles — `wishTile.packGroups`; a group is never split to fill a gap), and a wide list dealing its categories into column stacks (`categoryColumns`: one on a phone and at 1469, two at 1920, three at 2450). On a phone or a screen under 500px tall (a phone sideways, `useFoldedFilters`) the filters fold behind one Filters button, and Fold all is an icon at the end of that row (sideways the search shares that row); wider, the filters are one bar across the top with Fold all at its end (Wishlist and Owned) — never a line of its own above the list. While the Wishlist loads, its bar keeps its room only where it showed last time (`useRememberedFlag('shop:wishFilters')`; a first visit keeps it): an empty wishlist has no bar, and the bar leaving after the read moved the rail up. The sort selects say "Sort: Priority" (`SortSelect`: the box keeps the row's text size, the native select lies invisible over it).
- **Suggested categories and default pictures** (owner, 10.11.2026: "Add more sub categories and default pictures for them if no picture found or added"): pure `shopTaxonomy.ts` (`verify-shop-taxonomy`) holds ten top categories for a Norwegian shopper with 6–12 subcategories each — rain gear and wool layers, skiing, snow clearing and the cabin, pålegg, EV charging and roof boxes among them — in English and Title Case like migration 030's seed, which is included (they share one list with the owner's own, so it is never mixed). Names match case-, space- and "&"/"and"-insensitively and through a few other names for the same thing (`aka`: "Home" is Home & Living, "Gaming" is Hobby & Games, "Earbuds" is Headphones — a top known by another name keeps the picture its own words say, "Gaming" a controller), so a suggestion never repeats what the owner has and the owner's own names win. **One category picker** (`CategoryPicker`) in the add and edit sheet, the record's Category fact, Add something I own and the chain builder's steps: the owner's own categories first, then a "Suggested" group of the ones they don't have yet. Picking one only picks it (`suggest:<name>`; in a single category id, a pending one): **the sheet's Save makes it**, the way "+ New category…" works (`useResolveCategory`, owner review 10.11.2026), so Cancel leaves nothing and scrolling an iOS picker past a suggestion makes nothing; a suggested top offers its own subcategories at once. Save's toast says what was made — "Headphones added under Electronics · Undo" — and Undo moves what was saved in it to its parent, removes it and says so ("Headphones removed · 1 thing moved to Electronics"); it never steps back a saved record's draft, and it refuses before any write when the category holds a subcategory older than itself ("… was there before — nothing was undone", `api/shopCategoryApi.removeNewCategory` with the row's `created_at`). Nothing is ever added by itself, and there is no SQL to run. The filters list only the owner's own categories (a suggestion holds nothing to filter). **A thing's picture** is its own, else its price watch's, else its subcategory's default picture, else its top category's, else its initials on the category's tint. A default picture is a lucide icon drawn large on the category's tint in the 4:3 well (`.tint-well`, a soft fall of light like Apple's placeholders; never a stock photo or an emoji); a category the owner named gets one guessed from its words (`guessPicture`: "phone", "headphone", "jacket", "kitchen", Norwegian words too — "kjøkken", "sko", "sykkel"), else its top category's.
