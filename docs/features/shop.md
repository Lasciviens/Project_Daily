# Shop

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 13.10.2026 (word for word, apart from translated quotes). This file describes how the feature works and why. The rules Claude must follow while changing it are in `.claude/rules/shop.md`, which Claude Code loads by itself when a matching file is read or edited.

## Overview

See Shop section below. Wishlist (general wishes + models, deals, the Prisjakt price watch) · Quick list (Kassalapp chain prices) · Owned (what you own and owned, money chains, Try other prices, Stats). Its own nav entry (sidebar, More sheet).

## Shop Feature Detail
`/#/shop` → `ShopPage` (`src/features/shop/`), rebuilt 08.10.2026 (migration `137`) into three views, `?view=` (`bought` opens Owned): **Wishlist · Quick list · Owned** (Owned has **Things | Stats**, `?tab=stats`). Phones show no counts in the view control. A wishlist row lives a life: **To buy** (`status 'wishlist'`) → **Mine** (`'bought'`) → **Sold or gone** (`'bought'` + `disposal` facts). A POSSESSION = `list='wishlist' AND kind='item' AND status='bought' AND kept` (`ownModel.isPossession`); quick-list ticks are never possessions.
- **Categories** (Wishlist and Owned, `CategorySection`): full-width sections that fold ("Fold all"; remembered per device, `useCollapsedGroups`, `lasci.shop.collapsed.<view>`), each one aligned grid (`CARD_GRID`, rows stretch so prices and buttons line up — owner, 08.10.2026) with every card labelled by its subcategory.
- **Wishlist** (`WishlistView`, `WISHLIST_BOARD`): cards (`ShopItemCard`) show the picture (row's `image_url`, else the price watch's), the price now from the watch ("Now 6 990 NOK on Prisjakt · ↓ 300", "at your target"), a deal ("Deal from 14.10.2026 at Elkjøp · target 1 990"), "After selling RP5: 3 000 more" (projected plans), Need it / Just for fun; **Bought is one tap** (`useBuyNow`: today, the row's own price; toast **Add details · Undo**). **General wishes** (`kind 'general'`: a range `price_min/max`, must-haves `requirements` one per line) show as `GeneralWishCard` ("Any model · 5 000–8 000 · 3 models · from 3 990", Best match); their **models** are rows with `option_for` (never wishes of their own, counted once through the wish's range). Buying a model makes the wish `fulfilled` (trigger); its other models read "Not chosen" (`status 'dropped'`). The add sheet (`ShopItemSheet` md) takes a pasted link (`LinkReader` → `shop-price` `read` fills name, price, picture) and "One product | Any model that fits".
- **The record** (`components/record/ShopRecord`, xl, full-screen on phones) opens every wishlist-side row: state pill + one row of actions (Bought / Plan it / On the next errand / Not any more; Sell or give away / + Accessory — the only place for these two since 14.10.2026, the Owned cards no longer carry them; Undo: still mine), "Yours for 4 months · since …" for a thing you have, a **strip of four numbers** that depends on the state (`RecordStrip`; Mine = Paid · Could sell for · Final cost or Per month · **If sold now** — at "Could sell for", the chain's result when it is in one with this thing's own beside it, `chainModel.ifSoldNow`, the projection's rule: accessories riding with it go along, one without a value for nothing), the **facts as text — tap to edit, empty ones as "+" chips, yes/no facts (Bought to sell later, Not mine to keep, Next errand) as plain checkboxes, Save only when changed** (`RecordFacts` + `factList.tsx`, draft in `useRecordDraft`, closing with changes asks first; an open fact keeps its label in place and its Done tick where the pen was; an amount is one field — the number with a small plain currency dropdown at its end (`MoneyEditor`, also in the sell, own, accessory, model, cost and chain-builder sheets; owner 14.10.2026); **Accessory of** moves a thing under another — not offered while it has accessories or money links of its own), and beside them the **price watch** (`RecordPriceWatch`: source + date, trend, sparkline from `shop_price_points`, Check now, "500 less than you paid — return until …"), a general wish's **models compare** (`RecordModels`: price now, ✓/✗/? per must-have — one tap cycles `meets`, Best match = no ✗ and cheapest in range, Buy this model / Not chosen; pure `wishModel.ts`), **accessories**, **Money** (Paid with money from / Money goes to links, final cost, "after selling it needs X more") and **extra costs**. Quick-list rows keep the small sheet.
- **Header:** a still drawn shop shelf (`ShopHeroArt`, owner, 08.10.2026: "a background picture, not fully bright") — the page's header art (THEME.md §6.5), on phones too since 12.10.2026.
- **Owned → Things** (`components/owned/`, `OWNED_BOARD`): the filter bar runs across the top at every width (search, Mine · Sold or gone · All, sort; category or subcategory, money chain, bought to sell — folded behind Filters on a phone); the category groups come first and the rail sits on the **right** (owner, 14.10.2026): What I own, Coming up (return ≤ 14 days, complaint right ≤ 60 days). **What I own follows the list** (owner, 14.10.2026; `ownModel.shownSummary`, checked in `verify-shop-owned`): it sums the cards the filters and the tab show — Mine: things + accessories, Paid, Could sell for, "N sold or gone" (the folded list under it, counted only); Sold or gone ("What I had"): things, Paid (with the accessories that left in the same sale), Got back; All ("What I own and had"): both; "· filtered" when a filter narrows it. Stats keeps the whole-collection `ownedSummary`. The ways in — **Add something I own · Something I had · New money chain** — are only in the page's header row (buttons from a 72rem page; below that the first is the + button and the other two sit in its ⋯ menu, phones too). Cards (`OwnedCard`): "5 990 NOK · POWER" under the title, then label–value pairs: Owned (the day on hover or tap — `Peek`), Per month (only with a "Could sell for"; when it could sell for more than it cost, "Gained ≈ 429 NOK a month" instead — never a minus number, `ownModel.perMonthWords`), Could sell for (its date on hover or tap; warn + "update it" after 6 months), Final cost with "4 889 NOK + 1 138 NOK / lost on earlier things" under it; a gone card: Sold (etc.) after N months to …, Got back, Made / Cost you; then Chain · N, accessories inline. The ⋯ menu: Undo: still mine (gone), Money chain / Try other prices, Delete. A loss on a thing you use is neutral (its cost of use); success only for things bought to sell.
- **Popups** (`modals/ShopEntityModals.tsx`): `shop-sell` (How · On · To · Got, accessories ticked go with it — the money is split by what each cost (`splitSale`), unticked stay or move to another thing — Money goes to (+ how much), a live result line; one RPC `shop_record_sale`, toast Undo → `shop_undo_sale`), `shop-accessory` (quick add, "Add another"), `shop-model`, `shop-own` (backfill with "≈" dates, "Paid with money from", how it left), `shop-chain` (ledger with running net; **Try other prices** turns every amount into an input and replays the maths — nothing is saved).
- **Chain builder** (`shop-chain-builder`, `components/chain/`, owner 12.10.2026): a whole money chain at once — steps bought → sold → bought…, each with every detail (category/sub, store, country, currency, price, day, ≈/used/gift/to sell, notes, link, accessories that leave with it), how it left + Got, or "Could sell for" while still yours. **Add next thing** pays the new step with the last one's money (amount optional) and copies its category, store, country and flags live (editing one makes it the step's own; the currency is never copied live — a new step starts in the money's currency). Live numbers are chainModel's own on the rows the save writes (pure `chainDraft.ts`: `draftRows` live/save, `draftOutcome` — gained/lost, own money in, added along the way, back in hand; `verify-shop-chain-draft`), each foreign amount at its own day's rate (`fx_rates_nok`, else `shop-price` `day_rates` fetches and caches it; otherwise "rate pending"; a typed rate wins). Save = ONE `shop_restore_items` call (137's Undo RPC: rows parents first + links, one transaction under RLS, the triggers fill rates and check days) — a refused row leaves nothing; then the saved chain opens. Draft kept on this device (`lasci.shop.chainDraft[.<fromId>]`), closing asks first, a removed step has Undo. Entry: the Owned header's **New money chain**, Stats → Money chains, the chain popup's **Add what its money bought** (`fromId`: its last thing left). No migration.
- **Money maths** (pure, `scripts/verify-shop-owned.cjs` — incl. 4 000 random chains: identity, Σ shares ≤ 1, order independence, plans change nothing that happened, unknown spreads): every purchase/sale/cost is NOK **at the rate of its own day**, frozen on the row by the database (`fx_nok`/`sale_fx_nok`/cost `fx_nok`, Norges Bank's from `fx_rates_nok`, or typed = `manual`); missing = **rate pending**, never today's rate (`nokAt`); today's rates only for values of now (`nokNow`: Could sell for, prices to buy). An `Amount` is `{nok, missing, pending}` and reads "Unknown" unless complete. **Chains** (`chainModel.ts`) can be **named** (migration `138`, `shop_items.chain_name`: the name sits on the chain's thing that already carries it, else its oldest — `chainNameHolder`; two names after a join: the oldest thing's wins; `ChainName` edits it in the chain popup and the record's Money card; shown as the popup's title, on the cards' chain chip, in Final cost's hint, in Stats and the Owned chain filter — `chainTitle` = the name or "A → B → C"). Units = a thing + accessories riding with it (still on it, or sold in the same `sale_group`); a sold thing's result (paid + carried − got) passes on through links — an `amount` takes that share, the rest is shared by the receivers' prices — and what is not passed on ends there; **Σ paid − Σ got = Σ route cost held + Σ ended**. A link from a thing still yours or to a wish is a plan (`project: true` lets plans happen at "Could sell for"; `cashNeeded`). A link to a fulfilled general wish counts as its bought model.
- **Owned → Stats** (`components/stats/`, `STATS_BOARD`, `statsModel.ts`): a period picker (All time + each year) and a **Filter** sheet scoping the whole screen (`StatsScopeSheet` → `statsModel.scopeItems`: categories or subcategories, money chains, things, stores — OR inside a section, AND across; accessories follow their item; chains are built from every row, then kept when one of their things is in scope; the years follow the filter; active picks are removable chips); cards with their own filter: the timeline (Mine · Sold or gone · All), by category (Categories · Subcategories), stores (Spent · Purchases), cost of use (All · Still yours · Gone), chains (All · Going · Ended); in every drill list an accessory sits under its item (`drillNest.nestAccessories`, a muted header row for an item not in the list); tiles (net spend, what you own, cost of use per month, resale result or time kept), money in/out by month (diverging bars; only complete amounts drawn, the rest counted in a note), the ownership timeline (a bar per thing by category on a years axis), chains, by category (paid vs could sell for), where you buy, the dearest and cheapest per month, bought to sell — every tile, bar and row opens a drill list (`StatsDrillSheet`) whose rows add up to it.
- **Quick list** (`QuickListView` + `components/quick/`): "No rush" = `priority 'low'` (last in its store, off Daily); wishlist errands (`errand`) and deals due at their store (`quickFromWishlist`) join the store groups — ticking one buys it; grocery prices from Kassalapp (`food-search` modes `grocery_search` / `grocery_prices`; a row matched once keeps its `ean`, Buy again carries it), a basket per chain (`groceryModel.basketByChain`, "prices from DD.MM.YYYY"; REMA 1000 publishes no prices), scan to add.
- **The price watch + rates** (`shop-price` edge function, see Edge Functions): `shop_price_watch` (last read; a failed check keeps the last price and says why) + `shop_price_points` (history, 2 years) — written by the function only, never `shop_items` (no audit spam). The parser `priceRead.ts` (schema.org Product/ProductGroup/Offer/AggregateOffer, StrikethroughPrice, MerchantReturnPolicy; a Cloudflare check page = `blocked`) and `fxNorgesBank.ts` are HAND-MIRRORED into the function (checked by `verify-shop-price-read` and `verify-shop-owned`). The page asks for waiting rates once per 30 min (`useAutoFillRates`); the cron sweeps daily.
- **Data (migrations 137, 138)**: category FK now `SET NULL`; `status` + `fulfilled`; the columns listed in its header; one BEFORE trigger `shop_items_rules()` (currency from region, `bought_at`, refuses leaving bought while sold — "Undo the sale first", gone facts + day, both rates, parent rules one level deep, category from the parent); `shop_item_links` (+ `amount`, loop guard under an advisory lock), `shop_item_costs` (negative = rebate), `shop_price_watch`, `shop_price_points`, `fx_rates_nok`; RPCs `shop_record_sale`, `shop_undo_sale`, `shop_delete_items` (snapshot) / `shop_restore_items` (Delete's Undo brings back links, costs and children); 134's guessed `bought_at` rows marked `approx_dates`. `shopApi.ts` retries a write without 137's (or 134's) columns when only defaults are sent, and names the migration when the write needs them.
- Rules in pure `shopModel.ts` (`verify-shop-model`), `ownModel.ts` / `chainModel.ts` / `statsModel.ts` / `groceryModel.ts` / `fxNorgesBank.ts` (`verify-shop-owned`), `wishModel.ts` (`verify-shop-model`), `priceRead.ts` (`verify-shop-price-read`). Boards in `shopBoard.ts` (`verify-life-boards`).
- Daily's **Shopping** card shows only the Quick list (owner, 16.10.2026: "only show something I have added as buy next time"; 18.10.2026: "Quick list items only"): quick-list rows (not No rush), wishlist errands and deals due at their store (`quickDue` + `quickFromWishlist`), six at most then "+N more on the quick list"; a tick buys it with Undo (`useBuyNow`), empty reads "Nothing on the quick list." Wishlist plans for the day and top-wishlist hints are no longer on Daily.
- AI (`ai-proxy` `DB_CATALOG`): `shop_items` explains possession, selling (never delete; disposal facts, same `sale_group` for accessories), general wishes and models, and never writing `bought_at`/`fx_*`; `shop_item_links` and `shop_item_costs` are `rw`, `shop_price_watch`/`shop_price_points`/`fx_rates_nok` `ro`. It never invents a price, a sale price or a "Could sell for".
