===== health-science
1. Tone value: BenchmarkTone uses 'warn', not the 'warning' the brief listed. 'warn' is the app's own Tone name (src/shared/ui/Tone.tsx), so `<TonePill tone={c.tone}>` works directly. If another engineer coded a map keyed on 'warning', tsc will point to it.

2. Units classify() expects:
- VO2 max: ml/kg/min
- Resting HR: bpm. HRV: SDNN in ms.
- Steps: 7-day average steps/day. sleep_duration: hours per night.
- sleep_regularity: SD of wake time in minutes. This is a heuristic stand-in for Windred's Sleep Regularity Index.
- Body fat: %. BMI and waist-to-height: ratios (compute them with computeBmi / computeWaistToHeight).
- weekly_exercise_minutes: moderate-equivalent minutes (moderate + 2 × vigorous, via moderateEquivalentMinutes). strength_days: days/week. strength_minutes: minutes/week.
- Cardio recovery (Apple's cardio_recovery): bpm.
- Walking speed: km/h, as Health Auto Export sends it; converted internally.
- Respiratory rate: breaths/min.
- Blood oxygen: % or a 0–1 fraction; both are accepted.

3. Percentile means "the share of the reference population with a LOWER value". For resting heart rate a low percentile is therefore good; use BENCHMARKS[m].higherIsBetter to phrase it. HRV, steps, sleep and the other metrics without a population distribution return percentile null.

4. Nulls:
- classify returns null for NaN or negative values.
- Body fat returns null without sex.
- HRV returns null with neither a baseline nor an age.
- Blood oxygen returns null for readings of 0 or above 100%.
- VO2 without age and sex falls back to Kodama's generic bands with no percentile.

Example: 34.1 at age 42 is the 34th percentile on FRIEND but the 4th on HUNT3. Show it as clearly labelled secondary context next to the watch caveat, or leave it out. It is not the headline.
