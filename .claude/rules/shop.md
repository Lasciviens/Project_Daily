# Shop rules

Background: `docs/features/shop.md`.

- Every purchase, sale and cost is NOK at the rate of its own day, frozen on the row by the database; a missing rate is "rate pending", never today's rate. Today's rates only for values of now.
- A possession is `list='wishlist' AND kind='item' AND status='bought'` and kept (`ownModel.isPossession`); quick-list ticks never are.
- Selling never deletes: it writes the disposal facts (one `shop_record_sale` call, Undo `shop_undo_sale`); accessories sold together share a `sale_group`.
- Deleting a shop item takes the accessories bought for it (`shopModel.deleteScope`), the same from every screen.
- `shop_price_watch`/`shop_price_points` are written only by `shop-price` (Delete's Undo puts them back through `shop_restore_price_history`, 187), never `shop_items`.
- `priceRead.ts` and `fxNorgesBank.ts` are hand-mirrored into `shop-price` (their verify scripts compare them).
- Never read a site whose terms forbid automated reading: the list is data — `shop_site_policies` (188), a host or its subdomains — never a store name in code (owner, 10.11.2026). `shop-price` checks it before every fetch and every redirect hop, in `read`, `check` and the sweep, and fails closed when it can't read it (every row, page by page — a list cut at 1 000 would read a listed site); the answer is `not_allowed`, shown in plain words ("finn.no doesn't let apps read its pages").
- Read a page only where its site's robots.txt allows it — every site, every request (`read`, `check`, the sweep, each redirect hop), through the page fetch's own guards; never a site name in code. A disallowed path is `not_allowed` in its own wording ("prisjakt.no asks apps not to read this page", never the terms' wording); a robots.txt that can't be read means "not read now" (`error`), never "allowed"; a 4xx means no rules (RFC 9309). The rule and the link are compared in one form (an encoded unreserved character decoded, the characters the URL parser encodes encoded, hex in capitals), and more than `ROBOTS_RULE_CAP` rules is "not read now" — never rules dropped past a cap.
- One vocabulary for what a site refuses or fails, on every screen (owner, 10.11.2026): "apps" — never "robots rules" or "automated reading" — and never "open it yourself" right after the owner pasted the link. `shop-price` writes the site's answer (the watch's `error`, the read's `reason`); `watchWords.ts` adds what to do, the same for the add sheet and Add a model.
- Page text is kept as Delete's Undo keeps it (`keptText` in `priceRead.ts`): a name ≤ 300 characters, a picture only a web link ≤ 2 048, a currency only `^[A-Z]{3}$`; Prisjakt is told by its host, never by a text match on the link.
- A hostile page or robots.txt costs a moment, never the sweep (guardian review, 10.11.2026): every page text is bounded before a pattern runs on it (a price ≤ 64 characters, a path + query ≤ 2 048), the JSON-LD scan is one pass, robots patterns match piece by piece (no backtracking); `verify-shop-price-read` times each with the hostile input — keep those bounds when the reader changes.
- Each page is read at most once a day (the sweep, 20 h) and at most once an hour for what the owner starts: paste, save and Check now reuse the last hour's read (a watch of the same link, or the in-memory read by account and exact link) and say so — never a failed check (`error`: it read nothing, so it is tried again at once); a refused link has no Check now; a site's answer without a price is a warning toast, never a green "checked" — never a second fetch to "refresh". The sweep never asks again a link whose last answer was `not_allowed` while it is unchanged, and asks a `blocked` one again on the 7th Oslo day after (`BLOCKED_RETRY_DAYS` in `shop-price` and `watchWords.ts`, compared by `verify-shop-price-read`) — the card and the record say that day (Check now still may); don't list a store in `shop_site_policies` only because it blocks bots — that list is for terms that forbid reading.
- Prisjakt's price now is its new offers only (its own offer list; its schema.org figures mix in refurbished and used ones) — read from the stream's JSON rows only, never a raw text row (`<id>:T…`, which a user's review fills), and only when exactly one list holds this product's offers; otherwise its schema.org figures, marked mixed. Every watch and point says what its price covers (`condition`: new, mixed, unknown; NULL = before 188) — a later "lowest seen" compares like with like and leaves NULL Prisjakt points out (the record's history: `watchWords.historyOf`, a NULL Prisjakt point counts as mixed, and the ones left out are named); a price of another kind starts its own trend. When the offer list has no new offer, a kept price of another kind (NULL or mixed) goes — it is never left on screen as the price now — and the screens say "No new offer on Prisjakt", not "Couldn't check". `used_low` / `refurbished_low` are offers seen, never a "Could sell for". A Prisjakt price that may be a used one (mixed, or NULL) says so on every screen (", new and used"); a refusal and a page without a price are answers, shown in a neutral tone with the kept price's day — only a bot check or a failed check is the warning tone (`watchWords.ts`).
- Needs a look remembers a row only through `shop_signal_snooze` / `shop_signal_dismiss` / `shop_signal_put` (190): each answers the row before and after, and Undo writes `before` back exactly — never when `before` was a row the app can't read back (`beforeUnknown`: no Undo, read the list again; an Undo would delete it); a Got it's `signature` comes from `signalStates.signalSignature` and is data, only compared — never shown or followed. One row per thing, signal and `subject` (a deal day's slug: each deal day its own), unique with NULLS NOT DISTINCT — never a stand-in id.
- A category and a thing's category are the account's own (190's `shop_categories_rules`, `shop_items_rules` step g): a foreign key ignores row level security. The Undo of a category just made passes its `created_at` and refuses before any write when a subcategory under it is older (the cascade would take it). Price figures for many things are one `shop_price_stats` call (`usePriceStats`), never one history read per wish, and count clean prices only (`new`, `unknown`). Deal days are rows (`shop_deal_days`) worked out by `dealDays.ts` — never a date in code.
- A tile's belt and bar and Needs a look's price rows count clean prices only (`wishTile.isClean`: a new offer's or a shop's own page — never a mixed Prisjakt figure or a pre-188 read), so a used price never looks like a deal; a refused link shows no price from the link and the site's own words, neutral (`watchWords.watchNote`: "finn.no doesn't let apps read its pages"), a bot check when the morning check tries again — the same vocabulary as the record and the add sheet.
- Needs a look is one list for the whole Shop (`ShopNeedsALook`): the right rail on a wide page, one line that opens a sheet on a phone (owner, 14.10.2026 / 09.11.2026). On Owned it replaced Coming up, so a deadline is never listed twice. Every row has one action plus Snooze a week and Got it, and a row Got it hid comes back only when its signature changes.
- Categories are suggested, never seeded: a picker (`CategoryPicker`) lists the owner's own first, then `shopTaxonomy`'s "Suggested" ones; a pick creates it at once with a toast and Undo. Names match without case, spaces or "&"/"and", and through `aka` — the owner's own names win — so a suggestion never repeats what is there. A filter offers no suggestions (they hold nothing).
- A thing's picture: its own, its watch's, its subcategory's default, its top category's, else the initials. A default is a lucide icon on the category's tint, never a stock photo or an emoji; the icons and the taxonomy load in their own chunk (`useCategoryPictures`), so the Shop page keeps its 390 kB budget.
- Each subcategory is named once, above its own cards or tiles (owner, 09.11.2026; approved with the tiles, 10.11.2026): a label over every card cost a line each.
- Never invent a price, a sale price or a "Could sell for".
- A Kassalapp price counts (Cheapest now, a row's chip, the baskets) only when checked today or on one of the 14 Oslo days before — no date, no trust (`groceryModel.isCurrent`, every price through `byChain`) — and the basket says how many it left out and why ("4 old prices left out — not checked in 14 days", the chains on a tap): REMA 1000, Kiwi and Coop still come back with prices from 2024 (Kassalapp's FAQ, 10.11.2026).
- Stats follows the picked period (owner, 09.11.2026): a year is seen from 31.12 (today for this year), each card names it, and the definitions live in pure `statsModel.ts` (`periodOf`, `ownedAt`, `costOfUseIn`). "Could sell for" has no history: a past year knows it only for things still yours today — say so, never fill it in.
- A profit (made money, a chain ahead) is green — the success tone — on every screen (owner, 09.11.2026; before, only for things bought to sell). A loss on a thing you use is its cost of use: neutral, never red. A chain's running result has the header's sign: + ahead, − a net cost.
- Owned's summary is one line right above the list (owner, 09.11.2026 — no card): it sums what the list shows, the filters and Mine · Sold or gone · All (`shownSummary`), and a tap opens Stats on the same things (`statsScopeOf`). Mine · Sold or gone · All is a tab, never counted as a filter. Stats' "You own" keeps the whole collection (`ownedSummary`).
- Selling and adding an accessory live in the record, not on the Owned cards; Owned's ways to add live in the page header, not in the rail (owner, 14.10.2026) — on a phone in the row right under its bar, with Things | Stats, so the bar keeps one row on every view (03.11.2026).
