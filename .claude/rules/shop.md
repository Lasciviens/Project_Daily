# Shop rules

Background: `docs/features/shop.md`.

- Every purchase, sale and cost is NOK at the rate of its own day, frozen on the row by the database; a missing rate is "rate pending", never today's rate. Today's rates only for values of now.
- A possession is `list='wishlist' AND kind='item' AND status='bought'` and kept (`ownModel.isPossession`); quick-list ticks never are.
- Selling never deletes: it writes the disposal facts (one `shop_record_sale` call, Undo `shop_undo_sale`); accessories sold together share a `sale_group`.
- Deleting a shop item takes the accessories bought for it (`shopModel.deleteScope`), the same from every screen.
- `shop_price_watch`/`shop_price_points` are written only by `shop-price` (Delete's Undo puts them back through `shop_restore_price_history`, 187), never `shop_items`.
- `priceRead.ts` and `fxNorgesBank.ts` are hand-mirrored into `shop-price` (their verify scripts compare them).
- Never read a site whose terms forbid automated reading: the list is data — `shop_site_policies` (188), a host or its subdomains — never a store name in code (owner, 10.11.2026). `shop-price` checks it before every fetch and every redirect hop, in `read`, `check` and the sweep, and fails closed when it can't read it (every row, page by page — a list cut at 1 000 would read a listed site); the answer is `not_allowed`, shown in plain words ("finn.no doesn't let apps read its pages").
- Read a page only where its site's robots.txt allows it — every site, every request (`read`, `check`, the sweep, each redirect hop), through the page fetch's own guards; never a site name in code. A disallowed path is `not_allowed` in its own wording ("prisjakt.no asks apps not to read this page", never the terms' wording); a robots.txt that can't be read means "not read now" (`error`), never "allowed"; a 4xx means no rules (RFC 9309). The rule and the link are compared in one form (an encoded unreserved character decoded, the characters the URL parser encodes encoded, hex in capitals), and more than `ROBOTS_RULE_CAP` rules is "not read now" — never rules dropped past a cap.
- One vocabulary for what a site refuses or fails, on every screen (owner, 10.11.2026): "apps" — never "robots rules" or "automated reading" — and never "open it yourself" right after the owner pasted the link. `shop-price` writes the site's answer (the watch's `error`, the read's `reason`); `watchWords.ts` adds what to do, the same for the add sheet and Add a model.
- Page text is kept as Delete's Undo keeps it (`keptText` in `priceRead.ts`): a name ≤ 300 characters, a picture only a web link ≤ 2 048, a currency only `^[A-Z]{3}$`; Prisjakt is told by its host, never by a text match on the link.
- A hostile page or robots.txt costs a moment, never the sweep (guardian review, 10.11.2026): every page text is bounded before a pattern runs on it (a price ≤ 64 characters, a path + query ≤ 2 048), the JSON-LD scan is one pass, robots patterns match piece by piece (no backtracking); `verify-shop-price-read` times each with the hostile input — keep those bounds when the reader changes.
- Each page is read at most once a day (the sweep, 20 h) and at most once an hour for what the owner starts: paste, save and Check now reuse the last hour's read (a watch of the same link, or the in-memory read by account and exact link) and say so — never a failed check (`error`: it read nothing, so it is tried again at once); a refused link has no Check now; a site's answer without a price is a warning toast, never a green "checked" — never a second fetch to "refresh". The sweep never asks again a link whose last answer was `not_allowed` while it is unchanged, and asks a `blocked` one again on the 7th Oslo day after (`BLOCKED_RETRY_DAYS` in `shop-price` and `watchWords.ts`, compared by `verify-shop-price-read`) — the card and the record say that day (Check now still may); don't list a store in `shop_site_policies` only because it blocks bots — that list is for terms that forbid reading.
- Prisjakt's price now is its new offers only (its own offer list; its schema.org figures mix in refurbished and used ones) — read from the stream's JSON rows only, never a raw text row (`<id>:T…`, which a user's review fills), and only when exactly one list holds this product's offers; otherwise its schema.org figures, marked mixed. Every watch and point says what its price covers (`condition`: new, mixed, unknown; NULL = before 188) — a later "lowest seen" compares like with like and leaves NULL Prisjakt points out (the record's history: `watchWords.historyOf`, a NULL Prisjakt point counts as mixed, and the ones left out are named); a price of another kind starts its own trend. When the offer list has no new offer, a kept price of another kind (NULL or mixed) goes — it is never left on screen as the price now — and the screens say "No new offer on Prisjakt", not "Couldn't check". `used_low` / `refurbished_low` are offers seen, never a "Could sell for". A Prisjakt price that may be a used one (mixed, or NULL) says so on every screen (", new and used"); a refusal and a page without a price are answers, shown in a neutral tone with the kept price's day — only a bot check or a failed check is the warning tone (`watchWords.ts`).
- Needs a look remembers a row only through `shop_signal_snooze` / `shop_signal_dismiss` / `shop_signal_put` (190): each answers the row before and after, and Undo writes `before` back exactly — never when `before` was a row the app can't read back (`beforeUnknown`: no Undo, read the list again; an Undo would delete it); a Got it's `signature` comes from `signalStates.signalSignature` and is data, only compared — never shown or followed. One row per thing, signal and `subject` (a deal day's slug: each deal day its own), unique with NULLS NOT DISTINCT — never a stand-in id.
- A category and a thing's category are the account's own (190's `shop_categories_rules`, `shop_items_rules` step g): a foreign key ignores row level security. The Undo of a category just made passes its `created_at` and refuses before any write when a subcategory under it is older (the cascade would take it). Price figures for many things are one `shop_price_stats` call (`usePriceStats`), never one history read per wish, and count clean prices only (`new`, `unknown`). Deal days are rows (`shop_deal_days`) worked out by `dealDays.ts` — never a date in code.
- A tile's belt and bar and Needs a look's price rows count clean prices only (`wishTile.isClean`: a new offer's or a shop's own page — never a mixed Prisjakt figure or a pre-188 read), so a used price never looks like a deal; a refused link shows no price from the link and the site's own words, neutral (`watchWords.watchNote`: "finn.no doesn't let apps read its pages"), a bot check when the morning check tries again — the same vocabulary as the record and the add sheet.
- Needs a look is one list for the whole Shop (`ShopNeedsALook`): the right rail on a wide page, one line that opens a sheet on a phone (owner, 14.10.2026 / 09.11.2026; none on an empty wishlist). On Owned it replaced Coming up, so a deadline is never listed twice — and it lists what Coming up did (returns from 14 days ahead). Every row has one action plus Snooze and Got it; a snooze never hides a deadline past its day (a week, or the day before it); a row Got it hid comes back only when its signature changes. Money to claim comes only from an `ok`, clean read; a price not read today says its day.
- Categories are suggested, and added only on the owner's tap — never seeded, never by a migration (owner, 10.11.2026): a picker (`CategoryPicker`) lists the owner's own first, then `shopTaxonomy`'s "Suggested" ones (Title Case, as 030's seed); a pick only picks it — the sheet's Save makes it (`useResolveCategory`), never the select's change (an iOS picker scroll), and its Undo says what moved and never touches a draft. **Add the suggested categories (N)** — the Shop header's ⋯ (while there are any) and the first line of a picker's Suggested group, which only asks in a line under the selects (the same iOS reason) — makes every suggestion once through `useAddSuggestedCategories` (`suggestedPlan` from a fresh read of the owner's own rows, so nothing is made twice); its Undo removes only the rows it made, and of those only the ones still empty and as made (`removeAddedCategories`, the same `created_at` guard), and names what it kept. Names match without case, spaces or "&"/"and", and through `aka` — the owner's own names win — so a suggestion never repeats what is there. A filter offers no suggestions (they hold nothing).
- A thing's picture: its own, its watch's, its subcategory's default, its top category's, else the initials. A default is a lucide icon on the category's tint, never a stock photo or an emoji; the icons and the taxonomy load in their own chunk (`useCategoryPictures`; `useAddSuggestedCategories` loads the taxonomy and its writes the same way), so the Shop page keeps its 390 kB budget.
- Each subcategory is named once, above its own rows or tiles (owner, 09.11.2026; approved with the tiles, 10.11.2026): a label over every card cost a line each.
- Never invent a price, a sale price or a "Could sell for".
- Buy again comes from the owner's own quick-list ticks only (`buyAgainModel.ts`, pure, `verify-shop-quick`): three purchase days with gaps that agree (two gaps: the longer ≤ 1.5 × the shorter), a rhythm of 8 weeks at most, due from 80 % of the usual gap until one more gap (two weeks at most) has passed — never nag about things bought now and then, never while the thing is on the list by name or EAN — never a guess from outside data. One group leading the list, inside its box (it takes the skeleton's place — never a section that pushes a drawn list); a tap re-adds the latest purchase with "On the quick list · Milk · Undo", Undo takes that fresh row off (`useAddAgain`, shared with Bought before).
- A product's picture on a quick-list row is the row's own `image_url`: Kassalapp's `image` is saved only when the row has none (or exactly the old product's, on Change product) and only as a web link on a public host, made https, ≤ 2 048 — no user/password, IP, localhost, .local or single-label host (`webPicture`, the same in `food-search`) — no picture shows nothing, never a stock photo or placeholder.
- A scan asks `food-search`'s `grocery_ean` (`GET /products/ean/{ean}`), never the name search: the code only as 8–14 digits — the app (`scanEan`) and the function (`EAN_RE`, tested as sent, before the address is built) both refuse anything else; the function keeps `auth.getUser` + `isAppUser` first, and every field of the answer is checked where it enters (the `<grocery-ean>` block, `groceryHitOf`). Its shape is Kassalapp's own example, not a live sample — say so until one is seen.
- The basket's first line answers where the whole list is cheapest (`wholeList`): from the per-chain basket, so current prices only; it never calls a chain cheapest on rows it lacks, and says "N priced items" while rows are unpriced.
- The Quick list is its own chunk (`lazyPart` in `ShopPage`, preloaded in idle time) and the grocery hooks live in `useGrocery.ts`, so quick-only code never joins the Shop page's first download (390 kB budget).
- Resale (192): an ad's link (`ad_url`) is https only, then printable ASCII (one allow-list in the database and `resaleRows.AD_URL_RE`, checked on `scripts/fixtures/shop-ad-links.json`; the app sends `normalizeAdUrl`'s href), **never fetched** by anything and never written by the assistant — nor an ad's text (`AI_NO_WRITE`); a marketplace is a host (data), never a name in code (191's stores join it by host later). Sold is never written by hand: the sale (`shop_record_sale`) closes the thing's open listing and its Undo opens it again (`shop_items_resale`), and a listing's thing is checked when the transaction ends (Activity's Undo puts rows back newest first). A listing's price story is its own `prices` (a table pointing at a listing would be lost by Delete's Undo). The value history is the database's (every "Could sell for" with its day): a change takes back only the previous estimate's own row, never other days'. Text bounds count every character and need a non-space (`char_length(x) <= N and x ~ '\S'`, never `btrim`); amounts are below a billion. `resaleEvidence` counts only fresh rows with a NOK amount, never your own estimates or a "for parts" ad, and fills nothing in; ad templates are Norwegian, checklists English — both the owner's rows, read only for the assistant.
- Selling (step C screens): the suggested range only suggests — Use writes the record's draft, Save keeps it; nothing fills itself in. An ad's link and a marketplace's front page are links out that show their address (`AddressLink`: `webUrl`, no referrer) and are never fetched; a marketplace link is built from its host only (`marketplaceLink`). The ad is copied to the clipboard and never posted anywhere; its facts are the name, the condition, the day bought, the asking price (the listing's, else the owner's own estimate) and the accessories ticked — never a serial, the store, what it cost or a buyer. Sold is only the sell flow (`shop-sell`): a listing screen never writes Sold. A price cut is typed or a share of the owner's own price (`priceCuts`), never a price from outside.
- Hand-over ticks are a device note (owner, 11.11.2026; `lasci.shop.handover`, the last 20 things, by the step's words, cleared at sign-out): the database keeps the steps, the owner's rows; a step edit saves with Undo. New selling parts load in their own chunks (`lazyPart`) inside the record, never in the Shop page's first download.
- A Kassalapp price counts (Cheapest now, a row's chip, the baskets) only when checked today or on one of the 14 Oslo days before — no date, no trust (`groceryModel.isCurrent`, every price through `byChain`) — and the basket says how many it left out and why ("4 old prices left out — not checked in 14 days", the chains on a tap): REMA 1000, Kiwi and Coop still come back with prices from 2024 (Kassalapp's FAQ, 10.11.2026).
- Money back from a claim or a price match is a rebate on the thing — a negative extra cost (`shop_item_costs`), never a new money path — labelled by `complaintModel.REBATE_LABEL` (Stats counts it as saved through `rebateKind`), and its toast offers Undo (`useMoneyBack`, its own file so the Shop page's first download never carries it). The complaint letter is Norwegian content built from the thing's facts only (`complaintLetter`: each fact kept to one line); the complaint right is the noted day, else the law's two years from the purchase day — the earlier day when unsure. Something's wrong saves only the money back (owner, 11.11.2026): what's wrong and the letter stay on the screen, copied by hand.
- New vs used vs refurbished (a wish's record, `RecordOwnership`) compares only prices the Shop has (`ownershipPrices`): new from a clean read of the wish's own link, else its own price; used and refurbished only Prisjakt's offers on that read, in the same currency — offers to buy, never a "Could sell for". A net cost comes only from your own kept % (`ownershipCost`: your sales and trade-ins at each day's rate), never a guessed loss of value: no sale, no net cost, and it says why; with one price and nothing to say it shows nothing (`worthShowing`).
- Owned's ⋯ menu is drawn in a portal, outside the page's container queries: it lists Something I had and New money chain only while their buttons don't show (`useElementWidthRem` on their group) — a way in shows once.
- The insurance list's "new price now" is a clean watch price only (a new offer or the shop's own page); its CSV never holds a formula (`csvCell`: = + - @ get an apostrophe) and its print view shows only the list.
- Stats follows the picked period (owner, 09.11.2026): a year is seen from 31.12 (today for this year), each card names it, and the definitions live in pure `statsModel.ts` (`periodOf`, `ownedAt`, `costOfUseIn`). "Could sell for" has no history: a past year knows it only for things still yours today — say so, never fill it in.
- A profit (made money, a chain ahead) is green — the success tone — on every screen (owner, 09.11.2026; before, only for things bought to sell). A loss on a thing you use is its cost of use: neutral, never red. A chain's running result has the header's sign: + ahead, − a net cost.
- Owned's summary is one line right above the list (owner, 09.11.2026 — no card): it sums what the list shows, the filters and Mine · Sold or gone · All (`shownSummary`), and a tap opens Stats on the same things (`statsScopeOf`). Mine · Sold or gone · All is a tab, never counted as a filter. Stats' "You own" keeps the whole collection (`ownedSummary`).
- Selling and adding an accessory live in the record, not on the Owned rows (and since step A Undo: still mine, Money chain and Delete too: a row is one tap into its record); Owned's ways to add live in the page header, not in the rail (owner, 14.10.2026) — on a phone in the row right under its bar, with Things | Stats, so the bar keeps one row on every view (03.11.2026).
- Owned lists compact rows (step A, approved 10.11.2026), the same two lines every row — picture, name, what was paid (the record's Paid; extras stay in its Costs — review, 10.11.2026); its facts and at most one tone tag (`ownedRow.rowTag`) — so a column reads as a list; the tag comes from facts only (a snoozed Needs-a-look row keeps its deadline's tag), and "For sale · N days" / "Listed" comes from the thing's open listing only (`sellingModel.listingTag`, after a return window): never a tag without its data.
- A row's "300 NOK cheaper" counts like Needs a look's claim: an `ok`, clean read in the thing's own currency while it can still go back — never a mixed or pre-188 figure, never a price kept from a failed check.
- Owned's wide list deals its categories into column stacks of 21–40rem (`rowStacks`, never more stacks than categories): a content row is never wider than 40rem (THEME W2), and a parent with accessories is taller than its neighbours, so rows never sit in a row grid.
- From a board width of 60rem a tapped row opens its record in the rail's place (`RecordPane`, `OWNED_BOARD`'s `pane`; owner's plan 10.11.2026), sticky, as tall as the room it has on screen (its footer never below the screen), with its own scroll; the list keeps its tracks and its scroll; Back, × and Esc close it and Needs a look comes back; a change not saved asks before it closes or another row opens. A phone keeps the full-screen record — the pane is absent at step 1.
- One record, two frames: `useShopRecord` + `RecordBody` are the popup's and the pane's alike, so they never differ; a link inside a record opens through `useOpenRecord` (the pane switches, a popup opens a popup) — never `modal.open({ kind: 'shop-item' })` from inside the record.
- The Story (`recordStory.storyOf`) shows only facts the Shop has, each with its own day: "On the wishlist" only when the row was made on an earlier day than it was bought (a thing added as owned never was a wish), bought from `bought_at` only (never `updated_at`), no arrival day until step B's "Arrived" — never a step without its date.
- The nudge line is Needs a look's own row for the thing (`nudgeFor`, the rows still shown — a snooze hides it here too; none until its reads answered: nothing moves while loading), else the tidy-up's words, else "What could it sell for?"; its button only for what the record can do at once.
- The record's sections fold in place and the device remembers which are open (`lasci.shop.recordOpen`, a UI choice like the folded categories); the record opened at an old estimate opens Resale; Prisjakt's used and refurbished offers (of the thing's own Prisjakt link) show there in the evidence as shops' asking prices — they cap the suggested range — never as a "Could sell for".
- The Shop page's first download stays within 390 kB: Owned's Things, Stats and the side pane load on their own (`lazyPart`, `SkeletonTab`, preloaded in idle time) — a new Owned piece goes in one of those chunks, never into the Wishlist's.
- While Owned loads it reserves only what every outcome shows (THEME §5): the filter bar where it showed last time (`shop:ownedFilters`, a first visit keeps it), the summary line's remembered height (`shop:ownedSummary`), Fold all's place; the rows wait for the default pictures — the bar arriving after the read moved the list 0.164 on a phone.
